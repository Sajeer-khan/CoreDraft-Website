The problem we address

Purchasing becomes expensive
when nobody sees the queue.

A request is made in one place, approvals happen in another, supplier quotations arrive elsewhere, and purchase orders must be reconciled afterward. Delays become invisible until a project needs the material.

ProcureFlow offers a structured path from need identification to procurement completion, with the next responsible action easier to spot.

↗ Clearer purchasing accountability↗ Fewer disconnected request and approval records↗ More transparent supplier and order follow-through
