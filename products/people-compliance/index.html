The problem we address

Small teams should not spend
every month rebuilding payroll.

Employee records, salary components, attendance inputs, changes and statutory obligations often spread across multiple spreadsheets and manual registers. That's expensive to maintain—and makes reconciliation harder.

People & Compliance organizes repeatable administration into clearer records, period controls and defined responsibilities, with the option to tailor supporting company-compliance workflows.

↗ More consistent payroll preparation↗ Clearer separation of records and period activity↗ Less manual administration around company obligations
