CoreDraft Studio / Innovation roadmap

Fewer products.
More problems solved.

Instead of introducing a separate subscription for every business challenge, we extend the systems that already solve it. New products make sense only when the problem demands a genuinely different operating workflow.

The principle

Don't sell the same
work twice.

A business owner doesn't need separate software for project margins, customer collections, stock movement, supplier records and management reporting if those functions naturally belong in existing workflows.

Our roadmap now distinguishes genuinely separate product ideas from capabilities already addressed by QuoteDesk, ProcureFlow, Construction Control and the rest of the CoreDraft suite.

01 / Potential standalone products

Different workflow.
Different product.

These ideas merit further customer validation because their day-to-day work is not comprehensively covered by the current suite. No release date or public availability is implied.

✳
01 / After-sales and field service

ServiceDesk.

Maintenance without the scheduling scramble.

A potential purpose-built platform for equipment asset registers, recurring maintenance, technician dispatch, service requests, visit records and contract-based service commitments.

What makes it distinct: A recurring field-service operating model, not simply an invoice or work-completion document.

  • Equipment and customer asset history
  • Preventive service and visit planning
  • Technician dispatch and service evidence
  • Contract and response-time visibility
Proposed · Not commercially released

02 / Contract obligations and renewals

ContractDesk.

Know what you promised. Know what's due.

A proposed obligations-first system to coordinate renewal dates, warranties, deliverables, amendments, contractual responsibilities and the risks of commitments being overlooked.

What makes it distinct: Ongoing obligation management beyond merely storing a signed agreement or purchase order.

  • Obligation and milestone registers
  • Renewal and warranty deadlines
  • Responsible-party follow-through
  • Amendment and commitment context
Proposed · Not commercially released
