The problem we address

The work isn't lost at quotation.
It's lost between the steps.

Quotation files, approvals, purchase orders, invoices and delivery acknowledgements often end up in different folders or chats. Each handoff adds uncertainty and creates another place for a job to stall.

QuoteDesk connects the commercial record across the journey, making it easier to prepare, approve, issue, track and close work with clear ownership.

↗ One commercial record across the lifecycle↗ Fewer untracked handoffs and document revisions↗ Better owner visibility into wins, pending actions and closure
