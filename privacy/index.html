We want to learn which public website pages people find useful, without requiring you to accept optional analytics to read our content.

Updated: 24 October 2026. This notice describes the public website at coredraftstudio.com; it does not describe separately hosted customer SaaS deployments, which may have their own arrangements.

Who operates this website

CoreDraft Studio (Private) Limited, based in Pakistan, operates this business and product information website. For privacy questions contact intake@coredraftstudio.com.

Optional Google Analytics 4

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use public pages, such as the most frequently read guides and which site sections are useful. Our current website integration does not load the GA4 measurement script until you select “Allow analytics.” Declining optional analytics leaves the main website usable.

After acceptance, GA4 may set first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_* and process device/browser, approximate location, referrer, page path and engagement-related information. These are handled by Google under its relevant terms and technologies; the available detail and retention depend on our Google property settings.

We also measure an event when a visitor clicks an email or phone contact link. It is a contact intention, not proof that an email was sent or a sale was made. We do not deliberately send the recipient email address or typed message as an event parameter. We do not use this integration for Google Ads personalization signals.

Your analytics choice

When you first visit, the website offers Allow analytics and Decline buttons. The decision is saved in your browser's local storage as coredraft_analytics_consent_v1 to avoid asking on every page. Declining prevents new Google Analytics measurement from loading in this website integration.

To change the decision later, select Analytics settings near the bottom left of any public page. Changing a prior acceptance to a decline clears known first-party Google Analytics cookies on the current domain and reloads the page. It does not erase any information already received by Google; you may contact us with relevant privacy questions.

Other normal website services

Our pages are delivered using web hosting and delivery infrastructure. The browser may request fonts, styles or scripts from service providers used to render the website. These requests can involve standard network information such as IP addresses and browser details. The site may use essential operational/security processing independently of optional GA4 measurement.

If you send us an email, the information in that message is handled through email services to respond to your enquiry. Please avoid including passwords, regulated health records or confidential customer records in initial contact messages.

Data requests and updates

You can ask about website-related information we hold, request correction or raise a privacy concern using intake@coredraftstudio.com. We evaluate requests according to applicable requirements and the information reasonably available to us. We may update this notice when the site's services or analytics settings change.

Have a question about privacy?

Contact us directly. You do not need to enable optional analytics to use this website.
