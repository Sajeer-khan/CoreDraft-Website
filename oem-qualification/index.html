The Problem

When Good Equipment Needs Better Documentation

You've built the equipment and can explain how it works. But the buyer's quality team still needs clear specifications, installation evidence, test plans and defined responsibilities before accepting it for intended use.

Qualification expectations vary by equipment, industry, jurisdiction and intended use. Relevant documentation can support a customer's assessment, but no document package alone establishes regulatory acceptance, GMP compliance or fitness for use.

!

Typical documentation risk: incomplete specifications and test evidence can create additional buyer questions or delay equipment qualification review. This is an illustrative scenario, not a customer testimonial.

FAQ

Common Questions

What equipment types do you cover?▼

Pharmaceutical manufacturing equipment (tablet presses, coating pans, mixers, granulators, filling lines), HVAC systems (AHUs, chillers, boilers, cleanroom air handling), water systems (purified water, WFI, storage/distribution), cleanroom equipment (laminar flow hoods, isolators, biosafety cabinets), and laboratory instruments (HPLC, GC, dissolution testers, stability chambers).

How long does a package take?▼

Timing is agreed after reviewing equipment complexity, supplied evidence, required document scope and review cycles. An expedited option may be discussed where feasible.

Do I need to provide technical drawings?▼

Yes — we need equipment datasheets, P&IDs (if available), and functional specifications. The more detail you provide, the more accurate the qualification. If you only have basic specs, we can work with that and flag assumptions for your review.

Is the documentation specific to a target country?▼

We discuss the intended use, destination market and applicable customer quality requirements, then agree on relevant documentation references. Regulatory acceptability depends on the actual equipment, evidence and responsible authorities.

Can I get a sample before purchasing?▼

Yes. Contact us and we'll share a sample qualification package (TOC + one completed section + a blank template) so you can evaluate the quality and format before committing.

What format do you deliver in?▼

Review copies and agreed editable Word/Excel source files can be provided. Formal approval or signature must come from the appropriately authorized parties.
