A practical approach to better operations

CoreDraft Studio is the trading identity of CoreDraft Studio (Private) Limited. Our software portfolio addresses commercial quoting and approvals, procurement, construction project visibility, pharmaceutical quality workflows, payroll administration and related operating processes.

We believe owners should be able to understand what's happening without asking ten people for the same status update. Software should make important decisions and records easier to find, not create another layer of administration.

Business processes before feature lists

We start with the work people perform: what initiates it, who is responsible for the next step, where evidence is needed and what management must be able to see. Then we discuss which existing system fits, what needs configuration, and what would require further development.

Our public product pages show genuine, sanitized user-interface previews and an overview of capability areas. Sensitive implementation rules and customer records are not published. Detailed rollout scope is established through private discussion.

Our software areas

  • QuoteDesk — quotation-to-closure commercial operations.
  • ProcureFlow — purchasing and delegated approval workflows.
  • Construction Control — project, cost and commercial visibility.
  • PharmaDocs — electronic quality management and documentation.
  • People & Compliance — payroll and company administration.

Responsible product claims

QuoteDesk and Construction Control are used in business operations. Other products are discussed as on-demand customization opportunities. Capabilities, integrations and availability are confirmed case by case rather than implied by a screenshot.

Software used in regulated pharmaceutical operations requires appropriate risk assessment, validation and customer-specific controls before production use. We do not represent unvalidated preview software as independently certified.
