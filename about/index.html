About CoreDraft Studio

Business software
with a reason to exist.

We believe a business owner should spend less time collecting updates and more time making the decisions that matter. CoreDraft Studio builds practical, tailored workflows around that belief.

Who we are

CoreDraft Studio (Private) Limited is a Pakistan-based company focused on software for business operations and related technical and documentation workflows. Our portfolio covers commercial quotations, procurement, project operations, pharmaceutical quality workflows and people administration.

We started with operational problems rather than software categories: work disappearing between people, approvals stuck without visibility, project costs that are difficult to interpret and records scattered across files.

How we approach software

We begin by understanding who needs to do what, where decisions are delayed and which information an owner actually needs. We aim to make the daily experience clearer while maintaining appropriate controls and responsibilities.

Some software is already used in business operations. Other products are offered through scoped customization discussions. We describe those distinctions on individual product pages rather than treating every prototype or concept as a fully launched subscription.

What we show—and what we keep private

Our site includes real, sanitized historical interface images and sample-data previews for the existing product portfolio. These help potential buyers evaluate the user experience. They are not testimonials, third-party certification claims or proof that every optional module is enabled for every deployment.

When a system touches regulated quality records, payroll or contractual obligations, we discuss the necessary testing, validation and legal or professional review separately from the visual demonstration.

Where we work

We are based in Pakistan and discuss software customization and engineering-documentation engagements based on the client's workflow and requirements. Specific implementation, support and onboarding arrangements are agreed directly.

Tell us what keeps slowing work down.

We would rather solve one genuine operational problem well than sell another overlapping tool.
